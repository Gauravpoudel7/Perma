# GLOSSARY: PERMA Terminology

## Core Concepts

### Perpetual Options
Options contracts with no fixed expiry date. Instead of a single settlement event, they utilize streaming premiums and margin solvency to maintain the position indefinitely until closed by the user (liquidation is a post-Fair stretch; Fair MVP has none).

### CLMM (Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker)
An AMM where liquidity providers can allocate capital to specific price ranges (ticks) rather than the full price curve (e.g., Orca Whirlpool).

### The "Primitive"
In PERMA, a **Short Position** is the primitive. Creating a short position physically adds liquidity to the underlying CLMM, which then serves as the backing for any **Long Positions** opened against that range.

---

## Position Types

### Short (The Seller)
The party that provides the liquidity to the CLMM. They earn the streaming premium paid by the buyer and the trading fees generated by the underlying pool.

### Long (The Buyer)
The party that utilizes the liquidity provided by shorts. They pay a streaming premium to maintain exposure to a specific price range.

### 1-Leg Position
A position consisting of a single long or short contract. (Multi-leg positions, like spreads, are deferred to V1).

---

## Financial & Risk Terms

### Streaming Premium
The cost of holding a long position, paid continuously to the short provider. Calculated on-chain via an accumulator index.

### Collateral
Assets (SOL/USDC) deposited into the PERMA protocol to back a position. Collateral is not fully escrowed for the notional value but must satisfy solvency requirements.

### Solvency
In Fair MVP: a user's free USDC is at least what their open longs already owe in premium plus a margin for the next `long_margin_horizon_slots` — no price, no P&L ([ADR-0003](../adr/ADR-0003-fair-mvp-risk-model.md); component 09). The general `Account Value ≥ Required Collateral` form is Protocol V1.

### P&L (Profit and Loss)
The change in value of the option position based on the movement of the underlying asset price relative to the selected tick range.

### Tick
The discrete price point used by CLMMs to define liquidity ranges.

---

## Technical Terms

### CPI (Cross-Program Invocation)
A call from the PERMA program to another Solana program (e.g., the Orca Whirlpool program) to execute an action.

### PDA (Program Derived Address)
An address used by the PERMA program to store state (e.g., `Position` or `Market` accounts) deterministically.

### Adapter
A specialized module within the PERMA program that translates PERMA's internal logic into the specific API calls required by a CLMM (e.g., the Orca Adapter).

### Observation/TWAP
A time-weighted average of pool-recorded price observations, used to resist "flash" price manipulation. **Not available on Orca Whirlpool** — the client exposes spot only and the `Oracle` PDA is adaptive-fee state — and **not used by PERMA in Fair MVP**, which reads no price for risk. Part B, contingent on an external source.
